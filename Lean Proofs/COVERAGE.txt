FORMAL MODEL AND NOTATION

* Theorem 1 uses any finite nonempty ordered arm type, hence any K>=1. Its
  most direct policy type, GeneralOptimality.FiniteHorizon.Policy A N, has
  exactly N Borel sampling rules and one terminal rule on proper Gaussian
  states with arbitrary positive real precisions. For the manuscript N=T-K;
  draw index j=T-t counts the remaining draws after the current sample.
* Paper arms 1,2,3 are Lean arms 0,1,2 in the three-arm Bayesian/policy model.
  Counts.MeanPath and the event definitions keep the paper's one-based indexing;
  the conversions to the policy model are explicit.
* The main event is the revised event with coefficient 3 in Zi. It includes
  X, both Yi, both Zi, and the count caps. No older coefficient-4 event replaces
  this event in the revised main theorem.
* Odd budgets have T=2q+1, with q=T'. horizonConsistent t c includes positive
  integer counts, count 1 for arm 3, and total t-1. W also caps the other two
  counts by q. Results quantified over all such states cover reachable states
  and do not assume reachability as an unproved certificate.
* Actual rewards use independent full Gaussian arrays. Arms 1 and 2 have true
  mean zero; arm 3 observations are shifted by an arbitrary Delta>0. Empirical
  sample means, their update identity, and the projection onto the paper event
  are explicit. Frequentist and posterior-predictive laws remain distinct.
* Junpei.Policy.StatePolicy is a measurable randomized posterior-state policy with a
  fresh independent standard Gaussian seed at each action and recommendation.
  AEOptimal is defined by local Bellman action optimality and terminal
  posterior-mean maximization, almost everywhere in the seed at every proper
  state. It permits nonoptimal choices on the corresponding seed-null sets.
  The main bound quantifies over this full formal policy type, including arbitrary
  optimal tie resolutions. It is not restricted to the canonical selector.
* SequentialRiskOptimal is a separate actual-risk definition: at each proper
  positive-integer-count state it minimizes the posterior expected simple
  regret of the policy's raw-observation continuation against every feasible
  regular, jointly measurable raw-tree family using a finite independent
  Gaussian seed array. It contains no Bellman action equality.
  familyRisk_eq_joint identifies its risk as one actual latent/noise/seed
  integral. Lean proves SequentialRiskOptimal implies AEOptimal and gives
  a deterministicBellman witness at every horizon. The new main endpoints
  use the actual-risk predicate directly.
* FiniteGaussian/FinitePosterior use any finite nonempty arm set, arbitrary real
  posterior means, and positive real precisions represented by NNReal. Adaptive
  strategy trees must satisfy Admissible, the recursive integrability condition.
  External randomization is also covered for almost-everywhere admissible trees
  with integrable expected regret. The added EmbeddingExecution and
  EmbeddingRandomized modules also cover regular Borel raw-observation trees
  and independent probability-seed families, deriving regret integrability
  from Gaussian latent-coordinate bounds. FlatPriorInitialization/Product now
  prove likelihood normalization against Lebesgue measure and its exact proper
  product-Gaussian posterior. FixedGaussianFlatInitialization connects it to
  the actual continuation world and risk integrals. No probability measure
  over an improper flat prior is asserted.
* invPower n T means (T^n)^(-1). Bounds with a fixed positive coefficient give
  the paper's uniform Omega claims. Formal quantified horizon thresholds replace the
  phrase "for all sufficiently large T".
