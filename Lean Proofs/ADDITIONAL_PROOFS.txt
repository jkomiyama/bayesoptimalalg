LEMMA 13: BOTH POLICY EMBEDDINGS

EmbeddingDirect.lean embeds a retained-arm strategy into the full problem
and computes its actual expected regret. EmbeddingRaw.lean constructs the
reverse simulator: when the excluded arm is requested, it takes and discards
a real retained-arm dummy reward and supplies an independent imaginary
reward. It preserves the actual sampling budget and substitutes a retained
posterior-best terminal recommendation when needed.

EmbeddingMeasurable.lean proves the Bayes simulator is Borel; the execution
and randomization results prove its feasibility and actual risk comparison.
EmbeddingCoupling.lean identifies the Gaussian product laws and proves the
coupled risk bound. PolicyEmbedding.lean discharges all of these properties
for the actual policies and proves Eq. (13) and Eq. (14):
  Junpei.PolicyEmbedding.lemma13_loss_by_policy_embeddings
  Junpei.PolicyEmbedding.lemma13_actionLoss_by_policy_embeddings.

Scope: arbitrary finite arm sets with a retained arm, strictly positive REAL
precisions, real means, every finite horizon, and every retained forced arm.
No simulator-regularity, risk-equality, or coupling-bound hypotheses occur in
the final statements. This also extends the earlier integer-count endpoint.

LEMMA 19: SHARED SAMPLES AND REORDERED ALLOCATIONS

CommonSample.lean, CommonSampleLaw.lean, CommonSampleJoint.lean, and
CommonSampleIntegral.lean construct per-arm Gaussian posterior-mean innovation
streams with conjugate observation laws, prove order invariance of terminal
sufficient statistics, derive the terminal Gaussian gap law, and prove:
  Junpei.CommonSample.stream_integral_eq_recursive_risk
  Junpei.CommonSample.stream_integral_permutation.

Coupling19Counts.lean, Coupling19.lean, Coupling19Stream.lean,
Coupling19Rates.lean, and Coupling19Saturated.lean construct the allocation
couplings and identify each forced gain with the integral of a shared-sample
risk difference:
  Junpei.Coupling19.left_common_product_gain
  Junpei.Coupling19.right_common_product_gain.
This construction proves both individual T^(-176) lower bounds and both
saturated-arm T^(-178) comparison bounds:
  lemma19_individual_gains_via_coupling
  lemma19_saturated_left_gap_via_coupling
  lemma19_saturated_right_gap_via_coupling.

The orientation choice is explicit: rightTie for the forced-arm-1 comparison,
leftTie for the forced-arm-2 comparison. Each comparison uses one fixed
count-only rule on both allocations, as allowed by the paper's choice of
orientation. A separate Lean counterexample rejects the stronger demand for
one globally fixed leftTie rule across both comparisons. That counterexample
does not refute Lemma 19.
