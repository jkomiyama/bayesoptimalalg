LEAN-CHECKED COUNTEREXAMPLE: FOOTNOTE 14

Let sigma(T)=1/sqrt(T^3) and gap(T)=sigma(T)*sqrt(log T). Lean proves:

  * There is an explicit c>0 such that, for every integer T>=5,
      P[N(0,T^-3) >= gap(T)] >= c*T^-4.
  * gap is not O(T^-3/2).

The combined theorem is
  Junpei.ProseCorollaries.Footnote14.counterexample_to_necessary_scale.
The direct negation of the claimed necessity is
  Junpei.Footnote14Scale.polynomial_tail_does_not_require_paper_scale.

Thus O(T^-3/2) is not a necessary threshold scale for a polynomial tail
lower bound. This interprets the footnote's poly(T^-1) in the polynomial
lower-bound sense used by that discussion; no exact polynomial asymptotic
or polynomial-valued probability is asserted. The adjacent sufficient
bounded-exponent calculation is not refuted.
The manuscript's proposed enlarged-event improvement remains
explicitly unproved; the counterexample is not a proof of that proposal.

CORRECTED FOOTNOTE 14 CHARACTERIZATION (REVISION 3)

Junpei/Footnote14Characterization.lean proves the corrected statement in
  Junpei.Footnote14Characterization.corrected_footnote14_iff.

For every sequence d:N->R satisfying d(T)>=0 for all T:

  (exists c:R, c>0, exists p:R, p>0, exists T0:N,
     for all T:N with T>=T0, P[N(0,T^-3) >= d(T)] >= c*T^(-p))
  if and only if
  d(T)=O(T^(-3/2)*sqrt(log T)).

The constants c,p,T0 are chosen once for the threshold sequence and are
independent of T. The probability is the actual gaussianReal measure of the
tail event Ici (d T), and the right side is Lean's Asymptotics.IsBigO relation
at atTop. No monotonicity or additional regularity of d is assumed. This is
a polynomial lower-bound characterization, not an exact tail asymptotic or
a claim that the tail probability is a polynomial-valued function.

The proof chain includes both implications, exact Gaussian standardization,
and realExponentPolynomialTail_iff_eventually, which connects the explicit
positive real exponent to the eventual natural-exponent lower-bound predicate.
It also proves equivalence with the earlier lower-bound predicate valid for
all T>=5, after adjusting the positive constant over finitely many horizons,
and proves the original O(T^-3/2) scale sufficient.

The original counterexample remains valid and is retained. This new proof
does not alter the manuscript's text or certify the separate Appendix F
enlarged-event improvement.

EXACT SCOPE LIMITS

* The main algorithm is a posterior-state policy, as specified by Definition
  3. Its competitors include concrete full-history Borel policies. No theorem
  here claims that every arbitrary history-dependent minimizer itself has a
  posterior-state representation preserving every history correlation.
* The p.7 contextual assertion about a general non-Gaussian proper prior with
  finite first moment is not formalized as a separate general-prior Bellman
  theorem. The actual results concern proper product-Gaussian posteriors.
* Remark 1's cited SR/change-of-measure/Bayesian-rate extensions are not
  independently re-proved: the manuscript does not specify all algorithms
  and regularity hypotheses needed for those external statements.
* Auxiliary mock/coupling randomization is explicitly realized by independent
  Gaussian blocks. The risk bounds are checked in that probability model;
  a separate theorem compressing any such block into one scalar Gaussian
  randomizer is not supplied. Bellman lower bounds are proved for arbitrary
  independent seed spaces, and the main policy itself uses fresh scalar seeds.
* Conjecture 9 and explicitly unproved Appendix F suggestions are not turned
  into assumptions. Numerical illustrations and literature history are not
  mathematical proof targets.

These distinctions must accompany any presentation of the verification.
Compiler acceptance certifies each explicit Lean statement and its proof;
it does not automatically certify a PDF's text or erase the refuted footnote.
